## Operational checklist

- [ ] Privacy notice and terms drafted and reviewed by counsel.
- [ ] Grievance officer or Data Protection Officer appointed, with contact details published.
- [ ] Data-processing agreements signed with every vendor (Atlas, gateway, video, SMS, email, storage).
- [ ] Data inventory and retention schedule approved.
- [ ] Data-principal request process (access, correction, erasure, grievance) with response SLAs.
- [ ] Breach detection, assessment and notification runbook (Data Protection Board and CERT-In).
- [ ] Medical-record access and disclosure procedure, including emergency access.
- [ ] Clinical governance: credential verification SOP, complaint handling, prescription audits.
